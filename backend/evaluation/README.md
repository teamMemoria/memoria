# Evaluation set

- [`rubric.md`](rubric.md): how to score a conversation.
- [`scoring_template.csv`](scoring_template.csv): the conversations and their scores.

## The scores in `scoring_template.csv` are AI-generated

The scores in `scoring_template.csv` were **generated by an AI model, not by
people**. The scorer columns are labelled `ai_scorer_1` and `ai_scorer_2`. They
are two separate AI scoring passes, run independently under the rubric. Each
`scorer*_notes` column gives the reason for every score, with transcript line
numbers (`n=`).

**These scores are not human validation.** Do not present them as
hand-scored, as human labels, or as evidence that the automated audit agrees
with people. Agreement between `ai_scorer_1` and `ai_scorer_2` only shows that
two AI passes agree with each other, not inter-rater reliability between human
scorers. To validate the audit against human judgement, two people still need
to score these conversations under `rubric.md`.
